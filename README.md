# one-last-thing


Today is November 6th, nine days before one of the most important exams of my life.

I wrote all of this down because I needed somewhere to put my thoughts. Maybe getting them out of my head will help me breathe a little, focus again, and move forward.

I’ll add this message properly inside the game once I’m back home in Jordan.

I genuinely don’t know if you’ll ever read this, or if you’ll ever even open this link again. Maybe by the time you find it, you’ll have completely forgotten about me.

That’s okay.

I think part of the reason I wanted to leave this here is for myself too. Something I can come back to years from now whenever life feels heavy, just to remember how deeply I was capable of caring about someone, and that even the feelings that once seemed impossible to carry eventually passed.

If you somehow found your way here, then this is what I wrote the day after our story ended.


If you ever somehow find this one day, I hope enough time has passed for you to read it with a smile rather than sadness.

Today is the day after we ended things. It’s November 6th, nine days before one of the most important exams of my life, and if I’m being completely truthful, my focus is almost nonexistent right now.

I felt horrible today. Devastated, actually. I don’t think I’ve ever felt this kind of sadness before, which is strange considering how much I’ve already experienced in life.

I kept thinking about what went wrong.
I think part of what made it difficult for me to understand was that, especially in the beginning, I genuinely felt interest from you. There were moments when I thought we were slowly moving toward something real, and then at some point everything seemed to change.

My mind naturally started looking for explanations.
Maybe I rushed things after initially saying I wanted to take everything slowly. Maybe once I realized how much I actually liked you, I panicked a little and showed you a version of me that was more talkative, more emotional, and more intense than I normally am. Maybe you simply got to know me better and realized I wasn’t what you wanted. Maybe you were never as certain about us as I became.

There are probably a hundred different explanations I could come up with.
But eventually I realized that it doesn’t really matter which one is correct.

If something doesn’t work despite two people having the opportunity to know each other, then maybe, one way or another, they simply weren’t meant to continue that story together.
And I don’t blame you for that.

I never hated you for your decision, I don’t hate you now, and I don’t think I ever could.
You were genuinely one of the kindest people I got to know. A strong spirit, a beautiful soul, caring in ways you probably don’t even notice about yourself, and someone I truly hope life became much gentler with.
You were also the first girl I ever really let into this side of my life.

The first girl I genuinely liked.
The first girl who made me discover a completely different part of my personality that I didn’t even know existed. I never thought I would be the kind of person who remembers tiny details someone mentioned weeks ago, builds ridiculous birthday games just to make someone smile, waits excitedly for random conversations, or becomes genuinely invested in another person’s happiness.

You brought that side out of me.
And if things had gone differently, I think you were very close to becoming my first love.
Maybe I was already halfway there.
I don’t know what your life looks like by the time you find this.

Maybe you’re married now. Maybe you have kids running around and somehow stealing whatever little energy work left you with. Maybe your life looks completely different from the one you used to describe to me.
Whatever happened, I just hope you didn’t let those dark days convince you to give up on yourself. I hope you didn’t resign from everything you worked so hard for just because life felt unbearable for a while. And if you did choose a completely different path, I hope it was because it genuinely made you happier, not because sadness convinced you that you weren’t capable of more.

You deserved better than letting a difficult chapter decide the rest of your life.
I hope home became easier on you. I hope the things that used to keep you awake stopped feeling so heavy. I hope your family situation softened with time, and I hope you eventually found people and places that made you feel safe, understood, and at peace.
Most of all, I hope you’re happy.
Not necessarily richer. Not necessarily more successful. Not necessarily living somewhere far away.
Just genuinely happy.
I hope one day you woke up and realized that the phase you kept talking about was finally behind you, and that the person you thought you had lost somewhere underneath all that stress was still there.

And I hope you became proud of her.
I don’t know what you’ll remember about me by the time you find this. Maybe I’ll just be some random doctor you once met on Reddit. Maybe you’ll barely remember the conversations that felt so important to me at the time.
And that’s okay.
People feel things differently. People attach differently. People remember differently. There’s nothing wrong with that.
I don’t regret knowing you. I don’t regret the stupid jokes, Khaleesi, the long conversations, or caring about you as much as I did.

For a little while, you made an important part of my life feel different.
And I’ll always be grateful for that.
So if you ever find this months or years from now, there’s nothing you need to do with it. No message to send, no explanation owed, and no unfinished conversation hidden between these lines.

It’s just something I wanted to leave behind for the person who, for a short time, meant much more to me than she probably realized.
I genuinely wish you the best in whatever life you ended up building.
May life be gentle with you, Khaleesi.

Maybe you never will.
But if somehow you did… I hope by then, you’re happy.
