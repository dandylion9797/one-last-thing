# one-last-thing


Today is November 6th, nine days before one of the most important exams of my life.

I wrote all of this down because I needed somewhere to put my thoughts. Maybe getting them out of my head will help me breathe a little, focus again, and move forward.

I’ll add this message properly inside the game once I’m back home in Jordan.

I genuinely don’t know if you’ll ever read this, or if you’ll ever even open this link again. Maybe by the time you find it, you’ll have completely forgotten about me.

That’s okay.

I think part of the reason I wanted to leave this here is for myself too. Something I can come back to years from now whenever life feels heavy, just to remember how deeply I was capable of caring about someone, and that even the feelings that once seemed impossible to carry eventually passed.

If you somehow found your way here, then this is what I wrote the day after our story ended.

November 7th — The Pain Is Fading, But My Mind Is Getting Clearer.

Today is November 7th.

The pain is decreasing a little, but strangely, my mind is becoming clearer.

I think I finally understand why this hurts so much.

It's because I didn't imagine your interest. I felt it. You gave me reasons to believe it was real.

You told me I had the deen, the personality, and even the looks you were searching for. You made me feel like I was someone you could actually see a future with.

And then, the moment things started getting serious, suddenly our personalities were different?

What changed?

You told me I should get bored. That I shouldn't be so patient. That I shouldn't keep waiting.

And now I wonder if that was what you wanted all along. Maybe you were hoping I would eventually get tired and leave, so you wouldn't have to be the one to end things.

You told me my financial situation wasn't a problem. That our family circumstances weren't a problem either.

But when things became real, suddenly everything seemed to matter.

Was any of this ever serious to you?

I used to believe that nobody could be genuinely serious about someone they met online. Then I met you, and I allowed myself to believe otherwise.

Maybe I was naive.

The truth is, I was always afraid of getting emotionally involved with someone online.

There was always this thought in the back of my mind: if someone could meet me and talk to me this way, what would stop them from doing the same with someone else?

Maybe I was never the only person you were talking to. Maybe I was. I honestly don't know.

And perhaps I'll never know.

But that uncertainty was exactly what frightened me about online relationships in the first place.

And beyond all of that, I knew there was something else I couldn't ignore.

My religion.

I knew that getting emotionally attached this way, without a clear halal commitment, wasn't the path I wanted for myself.

I knew my intentions were serious. I wanted marriage. I wanted to make things right.

But having good intentions doesn't automatically make every step along the way right.

Maybe I crossed boundaries I should have respected.

Maybe I allowed my feelings to become stronger than my principles.

And sometimes I wonder whether this pain is the consequence of choosing a path I knew I shouldn't have taken.

Maybe I deserve some of this heartbreak for getting involved in something I believed wasn't halal.

But then again, I never intended to play with anyone's feelings.

I genuinely wanted you to become my wife.

I wanted something real, something honorable, something that would eventually bring us closer to Allah, not further away.

And maybe that's another reason why this hurts so much.

Because I didn't just lose someone I loved.

I also feel like I disappointed myself.

I always thought I was a smart guy. Someone who could read people, understand their intentions, and recognize when something wasn't right.

But apparently, when love gets involved, I become incredibly easy to fool.

Looking back, I feel like you wanted things to end the moment I became serious in September.

So why didn't you just tell me?

Why did I have to practically ask you to say it?

If I hadn't confronted the situation, how much longer would I have kept waiting, hoping, and investing my emotions in something that was already over in your mind?

And yes, I can't help wondering whether there was someone else. Maybe someone wealthier, someone whose circumstances made everything easier.

I don't know. And perhaps I'll never know.

But I ignored so many things that bothered me.

The way you spoke about your father sometimes. The way your career and increasing your income seemed important enough that you would consider moving abroad alone. The way certain conversations could trigger your temper so quickly.

Maybe none of these things made you a bad person. But they were things I should have thought about more carefully instead of convincing myself that everything would work out.

And then there were your replies.

God, those replies.

I'm a resident doctor. I work long hours, deal with patients, surgeries, hospital duties, studying, and travelling. I have one of the most important exams of my life approaching.

And somehow, I still found time to reply to you as soon as I could.

Not because I had nothing else to do.

But because you mattered to me.

You, on the other hand, could take days to reply.

I kept making excuses for you. Work, family, stress, exhaustion, anything that would make me feel better about the silence.

But maybe the explanation was much simpler.

Maybe I just wasn't that important in your life.

And that realization hurts more than I can explain.

You travelled to Qatar, and I was counting the days and hours, wondering whether you were okay, whether you had arrived safely, whether everything was going well.

I genuinely cared.

Then I travelled to Egypt.

I spent a week away from home. I was preparing for my exam. I performed one of the most important surgeries of my life.

And what did I get?

You were depressed. You were struggling. And somehow, you had even forgotten that I had travelled.

I understand that people go through difficult times.

But I was going through things too.

Did you ever wonder how I was doing?

Did you ever stop for a moment and think about me the way I constantly thought about you?

You walked away without even giving us a real chance.

Without asking enough questions to truly know me.

Without meeting me even once.

Without seeing whether the person you thought was right for you could actually be right for you in real life.

And that's what makes this whole thing so difficult to accept.

You didn't reject the person you truly knew.

You walked away from someone you barely gave yourself the chance to discover.

It's heartbreaking to realize how deeply I cared for someone who might never have cared for me in the same way.

But perhaps the hardest part is admitting my own mistakes.

I ignored what was happening because I wanted so badly to believe in you.

I kept choosing patience when I should have been asking myself whether that patience was being appreciated.

I kept giving attention, understanding, and affection, hoping they would eventually be returned.

And now I'm the one left carrying all this pain.

Sometimes I tell myself I deserve it.

That I brought this suffering upon myself by refusing to see what was right in front of me.

But maybe that's not entirely fair.

Maybe caring about someone sincerely wasn't my mistake.

Maybe my mistake was forgetting that I deserved to be cared for too.

And you know what's the saddest part?

I've tried to hate you.

I've tried to convince myself that you're selfish, that you manipulated me, that none of it meant anything to you.

I've tried to turn all this sadness into anger because anger seems so much easier to carry than heartbreak.

But I can't.

I just can't.

Despite everything, despite the disappointment, the silence, the unanswered questions, and all the nights I spent overthinking...

I still wish you all the best.

I still hope you find happiness.

I still hope life treats you kindly.

And maybe that's what hurts the most.

Because even after everything, I can't bring myself to wish you anything bad.

I don't know whether your feelings were ever as real as mine.

But I know mine were real.

Every word. Every moment. Every little hope I had for us.

And perhaps one day, when this pain is completely gone, I'll stop wondering why I wasn't enough for you, and start wondering why I kept accepting so little for myself.
